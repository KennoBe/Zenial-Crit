ZenialCritAmount.ttf, ZenialCritBlank.ttf, ZenialCritCount.ttf, ZenialCritPush.ttf and
ZenialCritCountParen.ttf - the amount fonts of ZENIAL Crit

These five files are Modified Versions of Noto Sans SemiBold (ZenialUI/Media/NotoSans-SemiBold.ttf),

Noto is a trademark of Google LLC. Noto Sans reserves no font name. The modified fonts carry their
own family names ("ZENIAL Crit Amount", "ZENIAL Crit Blank", "ZENIAL Crit Count", "ZENIAL Crit Push",
"ZENIAL Crit Count Paren") and are released under the same license, the SIL Open Font License 1.1,
reproduced below.

Changes (_dev/tools/build_crit_amount_fonts.py):
  ZenialCritAmount.ttf      every glyph but the figures 0-9 emptied (advance one font unit); each figure
                            moved to the right by 0.6 em, its advance unchanged; kerning, substitutions,
                            positioning and hinting removed; family renamed.
  ZenialCritBlank.ttf       every glyph emptied (advance one font unit), the figures too; shaping and
                            hinting tables removed; family renamed.
  ZenialCritCount.ttf       every glyph but the figures 0-9 and the comma emptied; those replaced by
                            solid blocks eight em wide. A classic kern table overlaps every pair of them
                            by eight em; other shaping tables and hinting removed; family renamed.
  ZenialCritPush.ttf        as ZenialCritAmount.ttf, figures moved right by 1.5 em, and "(" replaced by a
                            square of 0.05 em at the end of an advance of 16 em; family renamed.
  ZenialCritCountParen.ttf  as ZenialCritCount.ttf, and "(" replaced by a square of 0.05 em with an advance
                            of one font unit, kerned into each figure behind it by eight em; family renamed.
All five are built without a timestamp of their own: building them again gives the same bytes.

They are not fonts to write with. A line of the game's combat log, which an addon can show but not
read, is set in them so that only its amount is drawn.
